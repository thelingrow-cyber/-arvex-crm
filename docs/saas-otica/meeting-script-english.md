# Meeting Script — SaaS Pitch (English)
> Use this as a guide — speak naturally, don't read word by word

---

## OPENING (30 seconds)

"Hey, thanks for the time. I want to walk you through a SaaS idea I'm building — it's for the optical store market in Brazil. I'll explain the problem, the solution, and then I want to understand if you can help build it."

---

## THE PROBLEM (1 minute)

"So, optical stores in Brazil — we're talking about 40,000+ stores — they all use old ERP software just for inventory and invoicing. Nobody is helping them actually GROW.

Right now, when a client sends a WhatsApp message asking about lenses or prices — a human has to answer manually. When a client's prescription expires after 2 years — nobody follows up automatically. When a lead comes in at 10pm — it's lost.

The softwares they have today are good for management. Bad for growth."

---

## THE SOLUTION (1 minute)

"What I'm building is a Conversational CRM with AI — specifically designed for optical stores.

Think of it as an AI that works like a salesperson for the optical store, 24/7 on WhatsApp. It knows the client's history, their prescription, if their glasses are ready at the lab. It answers, qualifies, schedules appointments — automatically.

On top of that, a simple CRM dashboard showing which clients are at risk of leaving, whose prescription is expiring, and what needs attention today."

---

## THE MARKET (30 seconds)

"No one is doing this specifically for optical stores in Brazil. The generic tools — like HubSpot, ManyChat — they don't speak the language of an optical store owner. They don't know what a prescription is, what an OS is, what a lab integration looks like.

That's the gap. Vertical SaaS for a niche that's completely underserved."

---

## THE TECH STACK (30 seconds)

"The infrastructure I already have:
- N8N for automation
- Evolution API for WhatsApp
- Claude (Anthropic) for the AI
- Supabase for the database

The core engine exists. What I need is someone to help build the full product — multi-tenant architecture, frontend dashboard, and make it scalable for hundreds of optical stores."

---

## QUESTIONS FOR HIM

"So I want to understand your side:

1. Have you worked with N8N or similar automation tools before?
2. Do you have experience building multi-tenant SaaS products?
3. What's your stack — are you more backend, fullstack?
4. What would a basic MVP take in terms of timeline and cost, in your view?
5. Are you open to an equity or revenue-share model, or you prefer fixed project?"

---

## CLOSING

"I'm moving fast on this — I have a launch event planned in about 20 days with a partner who has an audience of optical store owners. The goal is to show a working demo and start getting paying customers.

What I need to know today is: can you build this, and would you want to be part of it?"

## IF HE ASKS THINGS YOU DON'T UNDERSTAND

Say: *"Sorry, can you say that again more slowly?"*
Or: *"Can you type that for me? My English is good but I want to make sure I understand correctly."*
