# Imagens conceituais geradas — ARVEX v3

As três imagens abaixo foram geradas pelo recurso de imagem integrado do Codex.
São metáforas editoriais, não registros de operações, clientes ou projetos reais.
O texto e a marca foram aplicados depois, no `build.cjs`.

## `patch-panel-editorial.png`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: editorial photograph for an ARVEX Instagram carousel about fragmented business operations
Primary request: a candid, believable close-up photograph of an old physical patch panel with several disconnected brass plugs and dark cables, showing an interrupted flow; no futuristic technology
Scene/backdrop: real industrial office maintenance room, plain dark mineral green wall
Style/medium: documentary 35mm film photograph, not a 3D render or concept art
Composition/framing: portrait crop with the panel occupying the lower two thirds and clean shadowed negative space above for editorial typography
Lighting/mood: warm side light, deep cool green shadows, restrained contrast
Materials/textures: worn metal, dust, cable rubber, scratches, slight natural grain
Constraints: physically plausible components, no people, no logos, no readable labels, no text, no screen, no glow, no floating objects, no excessive sharpness or cinematic AI polish
```
