# Prompt da imagem da capa conceitual

Modo: ferramenta de geração de imagem integrada (image_gen). A imagem foi gerada do zero; a tipografia e a marca foram compostas separadamente no projeto.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: original full-bleed background image for a vertical 4:5 Instagram carousel cover by ARVEX, a business architecture company. Text and brand graphics will be added separately.
Primary request: A visually striking editorial photograph of a large physical architectural model: several ivory plaster office rooms stacked at slightly misaligned heights, with a single continuous burnished-brass structural beam and walkway connecting the rooms into one coherent system. One bold, legible central silhouette; the installation looks hand-built and photographed, with physical seams, fine dust, plaster grain and realistic shadows.
Scene/backdrop: deep charcoal studio with a subtle warm olive cast, no horizon or hard division.
Style/medium: high-end real-world editorial photography of a practical museum installation, restrained analog film grain, believable camera perspective and materials, not a computer render.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5. The model is prominent in the upper 60 percent, with useful dark uncluttered negative space in the lower 40 percent for a large headline. Keep the brass connector visible at mobile thumbnail size. No important details in the lower text area.
Lighting/mood: directional warm side light, deep but detailed shadows, high visual contrast, cinematic but credible.
Color palette: charcoal, ivory, muted mineral olive, one small warm brass/gold accent. Avoid bright green, teal, pink, neon or blue.
Text: none.
Constraints: no words, no letters, no logos, no watermarks; no people, faces, robots, chips, circuits, brains, holograms, stock-photo office scene or glossy CGI. The image must have distinctive physical texture and not look like a generic AI visual.
```
