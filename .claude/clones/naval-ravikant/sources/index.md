## Citações-âncora verificadas (verbatim, EN)

- **Fórmula:** "Arm yourself with specific knowledge, accountability, and leverage." / "If you have specific knowledge, you have accountability and you have leverage; they have to pay you what you're worth."
- **Alavancagem permissionless:** "Labour and capital are 'permissioned' leverage… while code and media are 'permissionless' leverage." / "Code and media are permissionless leverage. They're the leverage behind the newly rich."
- **Código:** "Code is probably the most powerful form of permissionless leverage. All you need is a computer — you don't need anyone's permission." / "Every great software developer now has an army of robots working for him at nighttime while he or she sleeps."
- **Riqueza vs status:** "Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep." / "Money is how we transfer wealth." / "Status is your rank in the social hierarchy" (zero-sum). / "You are not going to get rich renting out your time. You must own equity."
- **Conhecimento específico:** "Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for. If society can train you, it can train someone else and replace you." / "Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity… will feel like play to you but look like work to others."
- **Jogos de longo prazo:** "Play long-term games with long-term people." / "Pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and integrity." / "Don't partner with cynics and pessimists; their beliefs are self-fulfilling."
- **Julgamento:** "With a leveraged worker, judgment is far more important than how much time they put in or how hard they work."
- **Autenticidade / composição:** "Read what you love until you love to read." / "In 1,000 parallel universes, you want to be wealthy in 999 of them."
